# Hackathon submission

Checked October 28, 2026 against the [official brief](https://youdotcom-oss.github.io/halloween-hack-2026/),
[submission form](https://forms.gle/2z9dKv9gvsP3cEjW7), and
[organizer agenda](https://luma.com/you-halloween).

The published submission deadline is **October 28, 6:25 PM Pacific**.

- Submit through the form; a deployed URL or a Git push does not submit the entry.
- Public GitHub repository with a README and reproducible setup.
- A **1–3 minute demo video URL**; see [the walkthrough](demo-script.md).
- Approximately 200 words describing the project, plus an explanation of the partner integrations.
- Team members (1–4), names/emails, and the form's contact/social/company details.
- Confirm the hackathon work was built during the event and uses at least one You.com endpoint, MCP, or plugin.

Track 3's stated minimum is one indispensable partner integration; the page header
names all four and the form asks how they work together. Ghost Links implements
You.com Search/Contents, CrewAI orchestration, Parasail inference and One actions.
Keep the demonstration accurate about which connections are configured and which
actions were actually run. AWS AgentCore is optional. No specific database or
deployment provider is required by the published brief.

The rubric weights innovation 25%, technical implementation 25%, UX 20%, impact
20%, and presentation/documentation 10%. Fake live results and hardcoded responses
are disqualifiers. Saved results in the app must keep their saved-result label.

The form and video still require the team's completion; no submission is made by
the deployment scripts.
